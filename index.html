Futures + crypto

Scalps ICE futures (CL,NG,GC,HG,SI,XAU) and Binance spot crypto (BTC/ETH/SOL) from one loop.

Risk discipline

Hard envelope: 0.25% per trade, 1.5% daily loss, 3% drawdown. Never raised without evidence.

◈

Paper-first

Runs over a real-token-kline feed, not synthetic curves. Paper trades are a ledger you can trace.

◆

Public & free

No paywall. Docs, code and results are here so anyone can read and fork the bot.

How it runs

1
Data

Live klines and ticks stream in; no lookahead, no synthetic curve fit.

2
Signal

Momentum + volume rules resolve each candle into a long/short/hold verdict.

3
Risk → execute

ATR-positioned sizing is capped, then the executor places the trade.

Transparency: every result you see here is paper-only and traceable. No live fills, no real money — and no third-party site or “free trial” that asks you for your broker credentials.
